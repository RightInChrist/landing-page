🏠Housing Projects

Our Mission

Everyone deserves a safe place to call home. Our housing projects work to address homelessness and housing insecurity through emergency shelter, transitional housing, affordable housing development, and home repair programs.

We believe that stable housing is foundational to human dignity and enables individuals and families to thrive.

Current Initiatives

  • Emergency Shelter Services - Immediate housing for those in crisis
  • Transitional Housing Programs - Bridge to permanent housing with support services
  • Affordable Housing Development - Building and renovating homes for low-income families
  • Home Repair Ministry - Helping elderly and disabled homeowners with critical repairs
  • Homelessness Prevention - Rental assistance and financial counseling
  • Housing Navigation Services - Helping families find and secure housing
