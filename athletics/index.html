⚽Athletics Ministry

Our Vision

Physical wellness and athletics provide opportunities for building character, community, and faith. Our athletics ministry uses sports and physical activity to develop teamwork, discipline, and healthy lifestyles while creating opportunities to share the gospel and build relationships.

We believe our bodies are temples of the Holy Spirit, and athletics can be a form of worship and witness.

Athletic Programs

  • Youth Sports Leagues - Basketball, soccer, baseball, and more for kids
  • Adult Recreation Leagues - Community sports teams and competitions
  • Fitness & Wellness Classes - Exercise programs for all ages and abilities
  • Sports Camps & Clinics - Skills development and character training
  • Athletic Mentorship - Coaches who disciple through sports
  • Community Sports Events - Tournaments, fun runs, and athletic outreaches
  • Adaptive Sports - Programs for individuals with disabilities
  • Fellowship Through Fitness - Group activities and accountability
