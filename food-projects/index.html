🍞Food Projects

Our Mission

No one should go hungry. Our food projects are dedicated to fighting hunger and food insecurity in our local communities and around the world. We provide immediate relief through food banks and meal programs while working toward long-term solutions through nutrition education and sustainable agriculture.

Following Christ's example of feeding the hungry, we serve those in need with dignity and compassion.

Current Programs

  • Community Food Banks - Regular food distribution to families in need
  • Hot Meal Programs - Daily meal service for the homeless and hungry
  • School Lunch Initiatives - Ensuring children have nutritious meals
  • Community Gardens - Teaching sustainable food production
  • Nutrition Education - Classes on healthy eating and meal planning
  • Food Recovery Programs - Rescuing surplus food from waste
