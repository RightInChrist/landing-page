Our Vision

Technology can be a powerful tool for advancing God's kingdom. Our software tools initiative develops practical applications and platforms to serve churches, ministries, non-profits, and communities. All our tools embrace the #StandWithJesus movement.

We create free and open-source solutions that enable ministry work and community service.

Current Projects

  • Church Management Systems - Tools for member management, event planning, and communication
  • Bible Study Apps - Interactive applications for scripture study and devotionals
  • Ministry Collaboration Platforms - Project management and team coordination tools
  • Donation & Fundraising Tools - Secure giving platforms for ministries
  • Community Service Platforms - Connecting volunteers with service opportunities
  • Educational Software - Learning management and curriculum delivery systems
  • Prayer Request Networks - Apps connecting prayer warriors globally
