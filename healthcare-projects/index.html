⚕️Healthcare Projects

Our Mission

Following Christ's ministry of healing, we provide healthcare services to underserved communities. Our healthcare projects bring medical care, wellness programs, and health education to those who lack access to quality healthcare.

We believe healthcare is an expression of God's love and an essential component of human flourishing.

Current Services

  • Medical Clinics - Free and low-cost medical care for the uninsured
  • Mobile Health Units - Bringing healthcare to remote and underserved areas
  • Dental Services - Preventive and restorative dental care
  • Mental Health Support - Counseling and mental wellness programs
  • Health Education - Disease prevention and healthy lifestyle training
  • Medical Mission Trips - Short-term medical teams serving globally
  • Medication Assistance - Helping patients access necessary medications
