📚Education Projects

Our Mission

Education is a powerful tool for transformation. Our education projects provide learning opportunities for all ages, from early childhood through adult education, with a focus on biblical truth, academic excellence, and practical skills.

We believe every person deserves access to quality education that develops their God-given potential.

Current Programs

  • Biblical Education - Bible studies, theological training, and discipleship programs
  • Tutoring & Mentoring - Academic support for students at all levels
  • Early Childhood Programs - Preschool and kindergarten readiness
  • Literacy Programs - Teaching reading and writing to children and adults
  • Vocational Training - Practical skills for employment and entrepreneurship
  • Scholarship Programs - Financial support for students pursuing higher education
  • Library & Learning Centers - Resource centers for community learning
